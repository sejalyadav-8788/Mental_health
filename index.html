Mental Health Awareness

Understanding Stress

Stress is your body's natural response to challenges. While some stress is normal, chronic stress can affect your physical and mental health. Common signs include headaches, fatigue, difficulty concentrating, and mood changes.

Anxiety vs. Stress

Stress is typically a response to external pressures and goes away once resolved. Anxiety can persist even without clear stressors and may require professional support. Both are valid experiences that deserve attention.

Self-Care Strategies

Regular exercise, adequate sleep, healthy eating, social connections, and mindfulness practices can significantly improve mental wellness. Even small daily habits make a difference.

When to Seek Help

If stress or anxiety interferes with daily life, persists for weeks, or leads to thoughts of self-harm, please reach out to a mental health professional. Seeking help is a sign of strength.

Mindfulness & Meditation

These practices help you stay present and reduce worry about the past or future. Even 5 minutes daily can lower stress hormones and improve emotional regulation.

Building Resilience

Resilience isn't about avoiding stress, but developing healthy coping mechanisms. Practice self-compassion, maintain perspective, and remember that difficult times are temporary.
